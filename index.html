Looking for a pharmacy nearby?

Find pharmacies around you or search for another area. Check the available details before making your trip.

Your browser asks for permission only when you choose "Use my location", and your location is used once. It isn't stored. Sharing location usually needs a secure (HTTPS) page or localhost. Searching sends your coordinates to the Overpass service to find pharmacies, and a typed place name is sent to Nominatim to find its coordinates.

Finding pharmacies near you...
Please confirm before you travel. Details come from OpenStreetMap contributors and have not been independently verified. Opening hours may be out of date, and we can't tell whether a pharmacy has a medicine in stock. Distances are approximate straight-line distances, not driving or walking distances. Call ahead when you can.
