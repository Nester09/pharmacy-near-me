About Pharmacy Near Me

Pharmacy Near Me helps people in Kenya search for pharmacies using mapped location data. You can use your current location or type a town or neighbourhood, and the site lists the pharmacies mapped nearby, nearest first.

Where the information comes from

Pharmacy details come from OpenStreetMap, a map built by volunteers, and are fetched live through the Overpass API. Typed places are converted to coordinates with Nominatim. Nothing on this site is made up or stored in advance.

Please keep in mind

  • Results depend on how complete and accurate OpenStreetMap is. Some pharmacies may not be listed.
  • Opening hours and contact details may be incomplete or out of date.
  • This website does not check that a pharmacy is open or has a medicine available.
  • Distances are straight-line estimates, not driving or walking distances.
  • Contact a pharmacy before travelling when you can.

This website does not give medical advice. Ask a pharmacist or doctor about your health and medicines.

In an emergency, call 999 or 112. Don't rely on this website.

Help improve the data

If a pharmacy is missing or its details are wrong, anyone can fix it on openstreetmap.org. Corrections help everyone who uses OpenStreetMap-based tools.

Your privacy

There are no accounts, tracking scripts or advertising. Your location is used once, kept only in the open page, and sent to the Overpass service to find nearby pharmacies. A typed place name is sent to Nominatim. Closing or clearing the page removes it.
